# EduBridgeX 🎓

**EduBridgeX** is a platform designed to improve access to **quality education, career guidance, mentorship, and opportunities for students in rural areas**.

Built for the problem statement:

> **“Ideate and implement a system to enhance the quality of education in rural areas.” — Government of Kerala**

## 🚨 Problem

Students in rural areas often face:

* Limited access to quality education and resources
* Lack of proper career guidance and mentors
* Language and communication barriers
* Limited employment opportunities
* Lack of awareness about scholarships and government schemes
* Fewer opportunities for collaboration and networking

## 💡 Our Solution

EduBridgeX brings education, guidance, mentorship, and opportunities together on a single platform.

### Key Features

* 🤖 **AI Career Counselling** – Personalized career guidance for students
* 💬 **Communication Chatbots** – Practice real-world communication scenarios
* 👨‍🏫 **Mentor Access** – Connect with suitable mentors
* 📚 **Courses & Study Materials** – Access quality learning resources
* 🌐 **Multi-Language Support** – Learn in a preferred language
* 🔎 **Smart Recommendations** – Discover relevant courses, mentors, and opportunities
* 💼 **Employment Opportunities** – Find relevant jobs and career opportunities
* 🎥 **Live Webinars & Blogs** – Learn from mentors and industry professionals
* 🏛️ **Government Schemes** – Find scholarships, grants, loans, and other benefits
* 🤝 **Community** – Connect, collaborate, and share knowledge
* 💳 **Course Payments** – Secure online course checkout

## 🎯 Our Goal

To **bridge the gap between rural students and opportunities** by making education, mentorship, career guidance, and financial support more accessible.

> **Learn • Connect • Grow 🌱**
